ABOUT US

Empowering the next
generation of scientists

Bulls Science Olympiad (BullSO) is an undergraduate student organization at the University of South Florida dedicated to cultivating STEM passion among middle and high school students. We host premier Science Olympiad invitational tournaments, craft high-caliber exams, and provide mentorship to future STEM leaders across Florida and nationwide.

By bringing together passionate USF students, alumni, and faculty, BullSO creates seamless competitive experiences, hands-on event workshops, and interactive learning opportunities for hundreds of aspiring scientists each year.
